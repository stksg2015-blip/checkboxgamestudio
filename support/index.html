Ringleap: common questions

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing ads again.

This can happen after reinstalling the game or moving to a new iPhone. Open the game, tap NO ADS, then Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't see or refund purchases ourselves.

How often do ads appear?

Never in your first two games, then one full-screen ad every three games, and never more than once a minute. Ads only show between games, never during a run.

How do I unlock more themes?

Themes unlock as your best score grows: one every 20 points, up to 180. Pick an unlocked theme from the THEMES button on the title screen.

I lost my best score.

Your best score, themes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the game removes them, and they can't be recovered. Your Remove Ads purchase isn't lost: restore it as described above.

How do I change ad tracking or ad privacy choices?

To stop apps from asking to track you, go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, tap PRIVACY on the game's title screen to change your ad consent choices. See the privacy policy for details.

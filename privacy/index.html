Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

In short

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account. We don't run servers that collect data from our games.
  • Your scores and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show ads. Where the law requires it, you're asked first.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Ringleap saves your best score, your chosen theme, your sound setting, how many games you've played (used to space out ads), and whether you've bought Remove Ads. This is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ringleap shows full-screen ads between games, using Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect:

  • device and app information, such as the iPhone model, iOS version, language and app version;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped;
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow it (see below).

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission. iOS asks whether Ringleap may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared and you'll see less personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EU, UK and Switzerland. Before any ads are loaded, you're shown Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads and the purposes they need. You can change your choice later from the game's privacy choices option.
  • Remove Ads. If you buy Remove Ads, the game stops showing ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The game only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on another device. We don't receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Information we don't collect

Our games have no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics of our own. We don't sell personal information, and we don't receive the data collected by Google or Apple in a form that identifies you.

Emails to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and keep it only as long as needed for that and for our business records. Our email is hosted by Zoho.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Because we hold no personal information from our games, most requests are best made to Google (for ad data) or Apple (for purchases), using the links above. For anything else, including information in emails you've sent us, write to info@checkboxgamingstudio.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how our games handle information, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's App Store release notes.

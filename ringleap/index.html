Ringleap

A dot circles inside rings that spin and change direction. Tap to hop it outward through a gap. Miss, and you hit the wall. Wait too long, and the centre catches up.

Coming soon to iPhone

How to play

  • Tap the right side to hop outward to the next ring.
  • Tap the left side to turn the dot around.
  • Go through a gap to clear the ring and score. Hit the wall and the run is over.
  • The centre slowly grows, so you can't wait forever for the perfect moment.

Themes

Ten colour themes unlock as your best score grows, one every 20 points. Choose your favourite from the Themes page.

Free to play

Ringleap is free, with a full-screen ad every few games. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns them off for good, and you can restore it on a new device.
